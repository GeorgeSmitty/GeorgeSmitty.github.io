# Product Requirements Document: University of Iowa Sports Passion Survey

## Overview

Build a short web-based survey focused on measuring passion and engagement with University of Iowa sports. The app collects basic demographic information and fan engagement data and displays aggregated survey results.

The application will have two main pages: a survey form and a results page showing overall response trends. All survey responses will be stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL database and deployed as an Azure Static Web App.

---

## Tech Stack

- Framework: Vite + React
- Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL)
- Hosting: Azure Static Web Apps
- Charting: Recharts

---
